Quizout Live Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026.

On your device

Quizout Live stores your followed teams and quizzers, selected account and event, appearance, and cached public quiz information on your device. The app does not require a personal account. Followed team preferences are also sent to the Quizout Live Activity service to provide background quiz updates.

Connected quiz service

Quizout Live retrieves public accounts, schedules, rosters, scores, and statistics from quizzing.live. Requests include identifiers for the account, event, or quiz you view. Your device connects directly to that service, which receives normal connection information such as your IP address. Its own privacy practices apply to the information it receives.

Live Activities

When enabled on your device, Live Activities display quiz information on the Lock Screen and supported Dynamic Island. The Quizout service hosted on Railway stores an installation identifier, followed team and event preferences, and Apple push tokens to start, update, and end your Live Activities. It sends quiz updates through Apple Push Notification service. These identifiers are used for app functionality, not advertising or tracking. Inactive registrations are removed after 30 days without an app registration.

Advertising and analytics

Quizout Live does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or developer analytics services. It uses quiz information to display scores, following summaries, statistics, and matchup estimates.

Your choices

You can change or remove followed teams and quizzers in the Following picker. You can change the app appearance and disable Live Activities in your device settings. The next app registration applies changes to followed teams and Live Activity settings to the remote service. Deleting the app removes its locally stored preferences and cache; inactive remote registrations expire after 30 days.

Questions

For help or privacy questions, visit Quizout Live support.
