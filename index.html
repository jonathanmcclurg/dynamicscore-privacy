DynamicScore Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026.

Information you enter

DynamicScore stores the seasons, teams, roster names, quiz results, notes, and preferences you enter so you can score quizzes and view statistics. These records are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, the app also uses your private iCloud database to sync coach records across your devices.

Connected quiz services

When you choose to download an event, roster, question set, or live clock from quizzing.live, the app requests that information from quizzing.live. The selected account and event identifiers are included in those requests. The service's own privacy practices apply to information it receives.

How information is used

The app uses your records to calculate scores, summaries, forecasts, and CSV exports. Preferences are kept on your device. The app does not use your quiz records for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Your choices

You can edit or delete seasons, events, and quizzes in the app. You can export statistics using the Share button. iCloud sync depends on your device's iCloud settings. Information already shared through a CSV export is controlled by the destination you chose.

Questions

For privacy questions or help with your data, contact the developer using the support contact on the app's App Store page.
