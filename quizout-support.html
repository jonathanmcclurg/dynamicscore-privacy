Quizout Live

Follow public Bible quiz tournaments on iPhone and iPad. Browse schedules, see scores and quizzer lineups, follow teams and quizzers, and explore event statistics.

Get started

  1. Select the tournament organization on the home screen.
  2. Choose a season and event.
  3. Open a quiz to see its scoreboard, or use the schedule grid to browse rounds and sites.
  4. Use the Following control to choose teams and quizzers, then save your choices.
  5. Open Event stats to compare team and quizzer results.

Live scores and clocks

An internet connection is required. Information comes from quizzing.live and depends on the event publishing updates. Completed quizzes remain available when no event is live. A completed quiz may have no running clock. Pull to refresh if information appears out of date.

Following and appearance

Edit your selections in the Following picker. Change light or dark appearance using the appearance button on the home screen. You do not need a personal account to browse public events.

Contact support

Open a Quizout Live support request on GitHub. Include your app version, device model, event, and a description of the issue. Support requests on GitHub are public; do not include passwords, private contact details, or other sensitive information.
